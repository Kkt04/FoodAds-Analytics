# FoodAds Analytics: Canonical Metric Definitions & Business Formulas

This document serves as the single source of truth for all business, financial, statistical, and operational metrics used across the FoodAds Analytics platform. Any metric displayed in reports, SQL queries, or dashboards must strictly adhere to the mathematical formulas and boundary conditions defined herein.

---

## 1. Top-Line Marketplace & Financial Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Mathematical Formula | SQL Representation | Units / Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ad Revenue (Platform Top-Line)** | Total fees collected by the platform from restaurant partners for sponsored placements. | $\sum \text{spend}$ | `SUM(spend)` | INR (₹) / ₹ Crores |
| **Attributed GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)** | Total order value generated by consumer orders directly attributed to an ad interaction. | $\sum \text{attributed\_revenue}$ | `SUM(attributed_revenue)` | INR (₹) / ₹ Crores |
| **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** | Ratio of consumer order value generated per rupee of advertising spend. | $\frac{\text{Attributed GMV}}{\text{Ad Revenue}}$ | `SUM(attributed_revenue) / SUM(spend)` | Ratio (e.g. `3.54x`) |
| **CPM (Cost Per Mille)** | Effective price charged to an advertiser for 1,000 ad impressions delivered. | $\frac{\text{Ad Revenue}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 1000$ | `1000.0 * SUM(spend) / SUM(impressions)` | INR (₹) |
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | Effective cost borne by an advertiser for a consumer click on their sponsored listing. | $\frac{\text{Ad Revenue}}{\text{Clicks}}$ | `SUM(spend) / SUM(clicks)` | INR (₹) |
| **CPO / CPA (Cost Per Order / Acquisition)** | Advertising cost incurred to acquire a single completed food order. | $\frac{\text{Ad Revenue}}{\text{Orders}}$ | `SUM(spend) / SUM(orders)` | INR (₹) |
| **AOV (Average Order Value)** | Average gross order size of consumer purchases driven through sponsored ads. | $\frac{\text{Attributed GMV}}{\text{Orders}}$ | `SUM(attributed_revenue) / SUM(orders)` | INR (₹) |

> **Important Business Distinction:**  
> * **Ad Revenue** is the *platform's* revenue (P&L top-line).  
> * **Attributed GMV** is the *merchant's* gross top-line sales.  
> * **ROAS is not merchant profit.** A 1.09x ROAS means the merchant earns ₹109 in revenue for ₹100 spent on ads. After deducting 30% food costs and 22% platform commission, the merchant experiences a net cash loss on that order.

## 2. Consumer Funnel & Engagement Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Formula | SQL Representation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Impressions** | Total count of eligible ad cards loaded into consumer viewport. | $\sum \text{impressions}$ | `SUM(impressions)` |
| **Clicks** | Total consumer taps/clicks on a sponsored listing card. | $\sum \text{clicks}$ | `SUM(clicks)` |
| **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** | Percentage of ad impressions that result in an intentional click. | $\frac{\text{Clicks}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 100$ | `100.0 * SUM(clicks) / SUM(impressions)` |
| **Orders** | Number of completed food delivery orders attributed to an ad click within the attribution window. | $\sum \text{orders}$ | `SUM(orders)` |
| **Click-to-Order CVR (Conversion Rate)** | Percentage of ad clicks that successfully convert into a finalized food order. | $\frac{\text{Orders}}{\text{Clicks}} \times 100$ | `100.0 * SUM(orders) / SUM(clicks)` |

## 3. Inventory Capacity & Yield Economics

| Metric | Definition | Formula | SQL Representation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inventory Capacity** | Maximum number of ad impression slots available on the platform, governed by organic diner traffic. | $\sum \text{capacity\_impressions}$ | `SUM(capacity_impressions)` |
| **Inventory Fill Rate** | Percentage of available ad slots that were successfully monetized by an advertiser. | $\frac{\text{Filled Impressions}}{\text{Capacity Impressions}} \times 100$ | `100.0 * SUM(filled) / SUM(capacity)` |
| **High-Utilization Day Share** | Proportion of operating days where a placement achieved at least 85% fill capacity. | $\frac{\text{Days with Fill} \ge 85\%}{\text{Total Days in Period}} \times 100$ | `100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN fill >= 0.85 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*)` |
| **Marginal CPM** | Clearing price assumed for unmonetized excess inventory sold under promotional discount tiers. | $\text{Current CPM} \times (1 - \text{Discount Rate})$ | Handled in scenario models |

## 4. Merchant Cohort & Retention Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Boundary Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Advertiser** | Any merchant account that generated $> \text{₹0}$ in advertising spend during a calendar month. | `SUM(spend) > 0` |
| **Cohort Month** | The earliest calendar month in which a merchant partner first recorded advertising spend. | `MIN(strftime('%Y-%m', date))` |
| **Tenure Month ($M$)** | The integer number of elapsed months between the cohort start month and the active performance month. | $M = \text{MonthIndex}_{\text{active}} - \text{MonthIndex}_{\text{cohort}}$ |
| **Retention Rate ($R_M$)** | The percentage of the original cohort size still recording advertising spend at tenure month $M$. | $R_M = \frac{\text{Active Advertisers at Month } M}{\text{Total Advertisers in Cohort}} \times 100$ |
| **Churned Merchant** | A merchant partner with an active recorded churn date during the operating window. | Identified via `churn_date IS NOT NULL` |

## 5. Strategic Segmentation Thresholds (`09_segments_to_grow_or_fix`)

To allocate operational resources, category-tier segments are classified into actionable strategic buckets based on their ROAS relative to the global marketplace baseline ($\text{ROAS}_{\text{global}} = 3.54\text{x}$):

$$\text{ROAS Ratio} = \frac{\text{Segment ROAS}}{\text{Global Marketplace ROAS}}$$

| Segment Classification | Criteria | Strategic Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| **FIX** | $\text{ROAS Ratio} < 0.70$ (ROAS $< 2.48\text{x}$) | Restrict bids, introduce minimum order size thresholds, mandate meal combos, protect merchant cash flow. |
| **GROW** | $\text{ROAS Ratio} > 1.20$ (ROAS $> 4.25\text{x}$) | Aggressively upsell inventory, expand placement slots, co-fund growth campaigns with merchant partners. |
| **MAINTAIN** | $0.70 \le \text{ROAS Ratio} \le 1.20$ | Maintain current bid floors and automated balancing. |

*Filter condition: Segments with fewer than 3 active advertisers are excluded from classification to eliminate sample noise.*

## 6. A/B Testing Statistical Framework

| Evaluation Role | Metric Tested | Statistical Test Employed | Decision Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Metric** | CTR ($\frac{\text{clicks}}{\text{exposed users}}$) | Two-Proportion Z-Test (Pooled Variance) | $\alpha = 0.05$ (Two-Sided), Statistically Significant Positive Lift |
| **Secondary Metric** | Orders per Exposed User | Two-Proportion Z-Test (Pooled Variance) | Directional validation of user intent |
| **Guardrail Metric 1** | Click-to-Order Conversion Rate | Two-Proportion Z-Test (Pooled Variance) | Must not exhibit a statistically significant negative decline |
| **Guardrail Metric 2** | Revenue per Exposed User (₹) | Welch's Two-Sample T-Test (Unequal Variance) | Must not exhibit a statistically significant negative decline |

### Statistical Formulas
* **Pooled Proportion for Hypothesis Testing:**
  $$\hat{p} = \frac{x_C + x_T}{n_C + n_T}, \quad z = \frac{\hat{p}_T - \hat{p}_C}{\sqrt{\hat{p}(1 - \hat{p})\left(\frac{1}{n_C} + \frac{1}{n_T}\right)}}$$
* **Confidence Interval (Unpooled Standard Error):**
  $$\text{SE}_{\text{diff}} = \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}_C(1 - \hat{p}_C)}{n_C} + \frac{\hat{p}_T(1 - \hat{p}_T)}{n_T}}, \quad \text{CI}_{95\%} = (\hat{p}_T - \hat{p}_C) \pm 1.96 \times \text{SE}_{\text{diff}}$$
* **Automated Decision Engine:**
  * **SHIP:** Primary metric wins significantly ($p < 0.05, \Delta > 0$) AND no guardrail suffers significant harm ($p < 0.05, \Delta < 0$).
  * **RAMP CAUTIOUSLY:** Primary metric wins, but guardrails show non-significant directional decline.
  * **HOLD / KILL:** Any guardrail exhibits a statistically significant decline ($p < 0.05, \Delta < 0$).
  * **DO NOT SHIP:** Primary metric fails to reach statistical significance.

Executive Insights Memo: Ads Marketplace Strategy
To: Ads Leadership & Commercial Operations | From: Marketplace Strategy & Revenue Analytics
Period: Jul 2025 - Jun 2026 | Backend: MongoDB foodads Aggregations (Seed 42)

1. Executive Summary: The P&L Paradox

Over the 12-month period, top-line platform ad revenue grew from INR 1.12 Cr/month (Jul 2025) to INR 6.32 Cr/month (Jun 2026)—a 5.6x expansion (+472% YoY), totaling INR 50.68 Cr with INR 179.54 Cr in attributed GMV.

However, our diagnostic shows that the growth is 100% volume-driven while ROAS remained completely flat at 3.54x. More critically, 28.2% of ad revenue (₹14.3 Cr) sits in category-tier segments with ROAS <70% of marketplace average (<2.48x). In low-AOV cuisines like Beverages (1.09x ROAS), merchants are losing money on every order once food costs (32%) and commissions (22%) are deducted.

2. Strategic Diagnostic Findings

Finding 1: The "Low-AOV Trap" (Beverages & Desserts)

Returns diverge by 5.1x across cuisines: Biryani leads at 5.64x ROAS and North Indian at 5.01x, while Beverages stagnates at 1.09x and Desserts at 1.64x. Clicks are not the problem (Beverages has high CTR of 3.83%), but because basket size is low (₹140 vs ₹380 for Biryani), fixed click costs (₹129 CPA) wipe out merchant gross margins.

Finding 2: Placement Yield Disparity (Cart Page vs. Home Banner)

Cart Page delivers our highest ROAS (5.80x), highest conversion (21.98%), and lowest CPA (₹53.90), yet remains 24% unsold. Conversely, Home Banner underperforms at 1.30x ROAS and ₹265 CPA (5x worse than Cart Page), while Search Top is at 90% capacity saturation.

Finding 3: The SMB Churn Cliff

Retention is stable through month 3 (~95%), but drops to 86% by month 6 and 72% by month 9. SMB merchants suffer 31.1% churn—10x worse than Enterprise Chains (3.0%).

Finding 4: Bestseller Badge Experiment

A/B test on 60,000 users confirmed a +18.2% relative lift in CTR (p = 0.0001) and +27.6% orders/user (p = 0.0235) with zero guardrail regression. Verdict: SHIP TO PRODUCTION.

FoodAds Technical Architecture & Methodology

1. Database Architecture (MongoDB Aggregation Framework)

The platform relies on a normalized MongoDB 8.0 schema running on localhost:27017. Collections are indexed on compound keys for optimal query execution:

  • advertisers: 300 documents (indexed by advertiser_id, category, city_tier).
  • campaigns: 448 documents (indexed by campaign_id, advertiser_id, placement).
  • daily_performance: 86,170 documents (indexed by campaign_id + date).
  • inventory: 1,825 documents (indexed by date + placement).
  • ab_test_users: 60,000 documents (indexed by variant).

2. Statistical Formulation for A/B Testing

Primary metrics (CTR, conversion rates) use a two-proportion pooled z-test:

z = (p_T - p_C) / sqrt( p_pool * (1 - p_pool) * (1/n_T + 1/n_C) )
95% CI = (p_T - p_C) ± 1.96 * sqrt( p_T*(1-p_T)/n_T + p_C*(1-p_C)/n_C )

Continuous spend guardrails evaluate user revenue via Welch's two-sample t-test without assuming equal variances.

3. Opportunity Sizing Methodology

Unsold inventory upside models capacity expansion over the trailing 90 days with price elasticity decay:

Incremental Revenue = Capacity_3M * ΔFillRate * CPM_current * (1 - DiscountRate) / 1000
  • Conservative: +3 pp fill uplift, 40% marginal price discount (+₹37.6 L / quarter).
  • Base Case: +6 pp fill uplift, 25% marginal price discount (+₹81.6 L / quarter).
  • Optimistic: +10 pp fill uplift, 10% marginal price discount (+₹152.0 L / quarter).
